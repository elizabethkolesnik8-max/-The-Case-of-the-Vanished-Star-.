🕵️ The Case of the Vanished Star

Welcome, Detective. The school play premieres in 2 hours, but the lead actor has vanished.

Your Mission: Find the actor, analyze the characters, and resolve the conflict before the curtain rises.

📜 Rules of the Investigation:
• You have 30 seconds to make a decision in each scene.
• Your choices affect your Empathy and Logic scores.
• There are 3 different endings based on your total score.
• Choose wisely: in conflicts, words can heal or destroy relationships.

Scene 1: The Briefing

You meet the actor's sister, Sarah. She looks anxious, with tired eyes and a messy hoodie.
Sarah: "Alex is very sensitive and insecure. He hates being criticized in public. He had a fight with the director yesterday."
A) "Don't worry, Sarah. Tell me exactly what they argued about. I will find him."
B) "He is just being dramatic and stubborn. Where did he go?"
C) "I understand you are worried. Let's sit down. What makes Alex feel upset usually?"

Scene 2: The Dressing Room

You enter Alex's dressing room. It's a mess. On the mirror, there is a note written in shaky handwriting: "I can't do this anymore. They don't appreciate me. I'm just a backup."
What does this note tell you about Alex's current state of mind?
A) He is lazy and wants to quit his responsibilities.
B) He feels undervalued and his self-confidence is broken.
C) He is angry and wants to punish the director.

Scene 3: The Rival

You meet Leo, the understudy. He is charming, has perfectly styled hair, and wears an expensive watch. He smiles confidently.
Leo: "Alex is just irresponsible and jealous. He knew I was better for the role. He ran away to ruin the play out of spite."
How do you react to Leo's statement?
A) "You are right. He is clearly just jealous of your talent."
B) "That's a strong opinion. But Alex is sensitive, not spiteful. Why are you so eager to blame him?"
C) "Stop talking. You are the main suspect."

Scene 4: The Director

You find Maya, the director. She is ambitious and strict, holding a clipboard, looking stressed.
Maya: "I just want the play to be perfect! Yes, I told him his acting was 'wooden' and 'unconvincing'. I am strict, not mean! He needs to toughen up."
What is the best way to de-escalate this conflict with Maya?
A) "I know you want perfection, Maya. But harsh words hurt sensitive people. We need to find him and apologize."
B) "You are a terrible director. You ruined everything with your bad temper."
C) "Just admit you were wrong so we can move on."

Scene 5: The Clue

You check Alex's phone (with Sarah's permission). His last message to a friend was: "Going to the place where it's quiet. Need to think."
Based on Alex's character (sensitive, needs quiet), where do you go first?
A) The old park bench near the lake. It's quiet and reflective.
B) The noisy central cafe where everyone can see him.
C) Leo's house, to confront him again.

Scene 6: The Discovery

You find Alex at the park. He is sitting alone, looking miserable. His messy dark hair is hidden under his favorite green scarf. He is crying quietly.
What is your first action?
A) Sit down quietly next to him, offer a tissue, and say: "I'm here to listen, no pressure."
B) "Alex! The play is in 2 hours! Get up, we are leaving right now!"
C) "Leo said you are just being dramatic. Is that true?"

Scene 7: The Confession

Alex finally speaks: "Maya said I was terrible. And then I saw Leo practicing my lines. He smiled at me. I feel useless and betrayed."
How do you validate his feelings and encourage him?
A) "It's completely normal to feel hurt when someone criticizes you. But your talent is real. Let's talk to Maya together."
B) "You shouldn't feel useless. Just ignore them and go on stage."
C) "Maya is just stressed. You are overreacting."

Scene 8: The Confrontation

You bring Alex back to the school. Maya and Leo are there. Maya looks angry, Leo looks smug.
How do you mediate this conflict to find a compromise?
A) "Maya, your words hurt him. Leo, stop smiling, we know you provoked him. Let's find a solution where Alex feels safe to perform."
B) "Alex, just apologize to Maya so we can start the play."
C) "Leo should be the main actor now. Alex is too weak."

Scene 9: The Resolution Plan

The tension is high. You need a final plan to save the play and the relationships.
What is the most constructive solution?
A) Maya apologizes for her harsh tone. Alex agrees to perform, but with Leo supporting him backstage as a friend, not a rival.
B) Cancel the play. The team is too toxic.
C) Force Alex to go on stage without any apology from Maya.

🎭 Case Closed
